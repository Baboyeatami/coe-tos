# Institutional assets and third-party software

The MIT license covers the authored skill instructions, documentation, example
assessment and Python tools. It does not grant rights to institutional logos,
trademarks, names or pre-existing form artwork.

`skills/coe-tos/assets/cjc-template.xlsx` is the supplied **Cor Jesu College,
Inc. — College of Engineering** Table of Specifications form, bundled at the
repository owner's request. Its banner, logo, document-control information,
institutional names and reviewer/dean placeholders are retained from the
supplied file. `cjc-template.pdf` is a native spreadsheet export of that form.
The owner supplied the form; no separate open-content license was supplied
for its institutional artwork. Those assets retain their respective owners'
rights. Their inclusion does not imply institutional endorsement of this tool.

Users can supply an alternative template and profile. CJC percentages and
template labels are profile-specific, not universal educational requirements.

Python libraries are installed separately and remain under their own licenses:
openpyxl (MIT), python-docx (MIT), lxml (BSD-style), pypdf (BSD-3-Clause).
Optional tools such as LibreOffice, Microsoft Excel, OCRmyPDF, Tesseract and
PyMuPDF are not included in the distributed skill. Consult their respective
licenses when installing or redistributing them.
