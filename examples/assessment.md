# Synthetic assessment blueprint — demonstration only

Course: Numerical Methods. Program: BS Civil Engineering, BS ECE, BS CpE.
This is a newly proposed assessment, not a transcription of the earlier EMATH 311 exam.
Two questions, Q1 and Q2, worth 50 points each. The scoring below explicitly
assesses recall as well as application and analysis. Total 100 points.

## Q1: spherical tank (Bisection / False Position)
## Q2: heat balance (Newton-Raphson / Secant)

Each question uses the following rubric. These subcriteria are published scores:

- A: Governing-model knowledge, 4 points. Recall and label the governing equation,
  symbols, units and supplied domain assumptions, without deriving a new model.
- B: Visualization, 6 points. Analyze the graph and justify starting values.
- C: First algorithm, 10 points: recall the update rule (2), apply it in the
  spreadsheet (4), analyze diagnostic results and compare manual checks (4).
- D: Second algorithm, 10 points: recall the update rule (2), apply it in the
  spreadsheet (4), analyze diagnostic results and compare manual checks (4).
- E: Verification and convergence, 8 points: apply substitution/error checks (4),
  analyze convergence and justify stopping tests (4).
- F: Written discussion, 6 points: interpret the physical result, compare methods,
  evaluate limitations and justify a choice.
- G: Video analysis, 6 points: critique graph-based choices, evaluate verification,
  and compare numerical behavior. Procedure narration alone earns no analysis credit.

R / U / T per question: 8 / 12 / 30. Overall: 16 / 24 / 60.
Institutional adoption is a separate decision; this example is for testing the tools.
